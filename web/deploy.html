03 / Networks

Check, deploy, confirm

Each chain is probed over its public RPC: P-256 precompile, honest blockhash, EIP-2935 history, and the CREATE2 factory's code and address derivation. Deploy unlocks only when the required checks pass and nothing is there yet. Testnets first.
