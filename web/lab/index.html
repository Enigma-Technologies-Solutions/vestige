Vestige protocol · co-signature lab

Two keys, one recent block, proof on-chain.

A signer signs a statement about the chain's latest block. A passkey co-signs it. Vestige.sol checks both P-256 signatures against the chain's own block hash and records the encounter for anyone to verify.

01 · 02 / The ceremony

Co-sign a block

Beacon: A is an unattended device key that re-signs block head − 1 every block, like a door reader or an IoT device. Handshake: A is a person whose passkey signs once. Either way A declares a window, maxAge blocks, inside what it signs, and B must co-sign within it. The contract sees a signer, a window and a cosigner.

A signs every block without a prompt.

Passkeys on this device

None enrolled

Handshake needs two different passkeys: one for A, one for B. On one device that means enrolling twice; with “Security key / phone” B can be your phone.

ABeacon
